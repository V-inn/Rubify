## Full description (max 4000)

```
Reading Chinese and stuck on a character? Rubify puts pinyin, with tones, right above the Chinese text on your screen, in whatever app you are using: your browser, messaging apps, PDF readers, e-books, or textbook pages in Samsung Notes.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap the accessibility button (or the Rubify Quick Settings tile).
• Rubify reads the Chinese characters on your screen and draws their pinyin above each one.
• A small bubble lets you refresh after scrolling, hide the pinyin for a moment, or close it.
• Your touches still go to the app underneath, so you keep reading and scrolling as usual.

MADE FOR LEARNERS
• Tone marks on every syllable (nǐ hǎo).
• Characters with more than one reading (多音字) get the reading their word calls for: 银行 yín háng, 行人 xíng rén, 长大 zhǎng dà, 长城 cháng chéng.
• Works in portrait and landscape, and in full-screen apps through the Quick Settings tile.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything happens on your device. Rubify has no internet access.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you read is stored or shared.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE
Rubify uses Android's AccessibilityService API to provide its core feature: showing pinyin over the text on your screen. It uses the API only to (1) react to the accessibility button, (2) take a screenshot of the current screen when you tap the button, the Rubify tile, or Refresh on its bubble, (3) draw the pinyin in an overlay that does not block your touches, and (4) close the Quick Settings panel when you start Rubify from its tile.
Rubify never reads the screen on its own, does not monitor your activity, cannot read or operate other apps' text and controls, and does not change your settings. Screenshots are processed on your device and discarded right away; no data is collected or shared. Rubify asks for your consent, with this explanation, before the service can be used.

Rubify is a study aid for Mandarin learners. It is not designed as an assistive tool for people with disabilities.
```
