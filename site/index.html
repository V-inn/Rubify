Pinyin on your screen, in any app

Tap the accessibility button, and Rubify writes the pinyin above the Chinese characters you are reading: in your browser, chats, PDFs or textbook pages.

  • Tone marks, word-aware. Characters with several readings get the one their word needs: 银行 yín háng, 行人 xíng rén.
  • Stays out of the way. Your touches reach the app underneath; a small bubble refreshes the pinyin after you scroll, hides it or closes it.
  • Private. Everything happens on your device. Rubify has no internet access and collects nothing.
Download for Android Source code

Android 11 or newer. Open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Install blocked? In some countries, Google Play Protect blocks installing apps that use an accessibility service from a download. Until Rubify is on Google Play, install it from a computer with adb install. See how.
